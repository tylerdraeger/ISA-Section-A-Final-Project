Reports 2026 Third Quarter ResultsDOWNERS GROVE, IL – October 27, 2026 – InvenTrust Properties Corp. (“InvenTrust” or the “Company”) (NYSE: IVT) today reported financial and operating results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. For the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, the Company reported Net Income of $19.4 million, or $0.25 per diluted share, and $6.0 million, or $0.08 per diluted share, respectively. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, the Company reported Net Income of $26.0 million, or $0.33 per diluted share, and $108.8 million, or $1.39 per diluted share, respectively. Third Quarter 2026 Highlights:•Nareit FFO of $0.53 per diluted share•Core FFO of $0.50 per diluted share•Same Property Net Operating Income (“NOI”) growth of 3.1%•Leased Occupancy as of September 30, 2026 of 96.4%•Executed 86 leases totaling approximately 549,000 square feet of GLA, of which 512,000 square feet was executed at a blended comparable lease spread of 14.2%•Acquired one property, totaling approximately 169,000 square feet, for a purchase price of $34.0 million“InvenTrust made meaningful progress during the quarter, converting signed leases into rent-paying occupancy and cash flow,” said DJ Busch, President and Chief Executive Officer of InvenTrust. “Our internal growth drivers, together with the earnings contribution from recent acquisitions, helped drive year-to-date Nareit FFO per-share growth of approximately 10%. As we look toward 2027, our signed-but-not-open leases, redevelopment activity and acquisition opportunities provide multiple avenues to grow the business and create long-term shareholder value.”NET INCOME•Net Income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $19.4 million, or $0.25 per diluted share, compared to $6.0 million, or $0.08 per diluted share, for the same period in 2025. •Net Income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $26.0 million, or $0.33 per diluted share, compared to $108.8 million, or $1.39 per diluted share, for the same period in 2025.NAREIT FFO•Nareit FFO for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $41.5 million, or $0.53 per diluted share, compared to $38.4 million, or $0.49 per diluted share, for the same period in 2025.•Nareit FFO for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $122.6 million, or $1.56 per diluted share, compared to $111.1 million, or $1.42 per diluted share, for the same period in 2025. 1 Earnings Release - NOI excludes general and administrative expenses, depreciation and amortization, other income and expense, net, impairment of real estate assets, gains (losses) from sales of properties, gains (losses) on extinguishment of debt, interest expense, net, lease termination income and expense, and GAAP rent adjustments such as amortization of market-lease intangibles, amortization of lease incentives, and straight-line rent adjustments (“GAAP Rent Adjustments”). The Company bifurcates NOI into Same Property NOI and NOI from other investment properties based on whether the retail properties meet the Company’s Same Property criteria. NOI from other investment properties includes adjustments for the Company’s captive insurance company.NAREIT FUNDS FROM OPERATIONS (NAREIT FFO) and CORE FFOThe Company’s non-GAAP measure of Nareit Funds From Operations ("Nareit FFO"), based on the National Association of Real Estate Investment Trusts ("Nareit") definition, is net income (or loss) in accordance with GAAP, excluding gains (or losses) resulting from dispositions of properties, plus depreciation and amortization and impairment charges on depreciable real property. Core Funds From Operations (“Core FFO”) is an additional supplemental non-GAAP financial measure of the Company’s operating performance. In particular, Core FFO provides an additional measure to compare the operating performance of different REITs without having to account for certain remaining amortization assumptions within Nareit FFO and other unique revenue and expense items which some may consider not pertinent to measuring a particular company’s ongoing operating performance.EARNINGS BEFORE INTEREST, TAXES, DEPRECIATION, AND AMORTIZATION (EBITDA) and ADJUSTED EBITDAThe Company’s non-GAAP measure of EBITDA is net income (or loss) in accordance with GAAP, excluding interest expense, net, income tax expense (or benefit), and depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA is an additional supplemental non-GAAP financial measure of the Company’s operating performance. In particular, Adjusted EBITDA provides an additional measure to compare the operating performance of different REITs without having to account for certain remaining amortization assumptions within EBITDA, certain gains or losses remaining within EBITDA, and other unique revenue and expense items which some may consider not pertinent to measuring a particular company's ongoing operating performance.NET DEBT-TO-ADJUSTED EBITDA Net Debt-to-Adjusted EBITDA is Net Debt divided by Adjusted EBITDA. 4 Earnings Release - Quarter Ended September 30, 2026About InvenTrust Properties Corp.InvenTrust Properties Corp. (the “Company,” "IVT," or "InvenTrust") is a premier Sun Belt, multi-tenant essential retail REIT that owns, leases, redevelops, acquires and manages grocery-anchored neighborhood and community centers as well as high-quality power centers that often have a grocery component. Management pursues the Company's business strategy by acquiring retail properties in Sun Belt markets, opportunistically disposing of retail properties, and maintaining a flexible capital structure. A trusted, local operator bringing real estate expertise to its tenant relationships, IVT has built a strong reputation with market participants across its portfolio.
