Reports Third Quarter 2026 Earnings November 5, 2026, Bethesda, MD. Saul Centers, Inc. (NYSE: BFS) (the "Company"), an equity real estate investment trust ("REIT"), announced operating results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 ("2026 Quarter"). Total revenue for the 2026 Quarter increased to $78.4 million from $72.0 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025 ("2025 Quarter"). Net income decreased to $12.6 million for the 2026 Quarter from $14.0 million for the 2025 Quarter. During the 2026 Quarter, the Company continued to lease residential units at Hampden House. As of November 2, 2026, 267 (73.0%) of the 366 residential units were leased and occupied and 100% of the retail space was leased. Concurrent with the opening of Hampden House on October 1, 2025, interest, real estate taxes, depreciation and all other costs associated with the residential portion and the majority of the retail portion of the property began to be charged to expense, while revenue continues to grow as occupancy increases. As a result, compared to the 2025 Quarter, net income for the 2026 Quarter was adversely impacted by $3.7 million due to the initial operations of Hampden House, of which $2.9 million is related to interest expense, net. Exclusive of Hampden House, net income increased by $2.3 million primarily due to higher commercial base rent of $2.0 million. Net income available to common stockholders decreased to $6.7 million, or $0.27 per basic and diluted share, for the 2026 Quarter from $7.7 million, or $0.32 per basic and diluted share, for the 2025 Quarter. Compared to the 2025 Quarter, net income available to common stockholders for the 2026 Quarter was adversely impacted by $1.6 million, or $0.07 per basic and diluted share, due to the initial operations of Hampden House.Same property revenue increased $3.5 million, or 4.9%, and same property net operating income increased $2.5 million, or 5.0%, for the 2026 Quarter compared to the 2025 Quarter. Same property revenue was favorably impacted by $1.2 million due to the lease up of Twinbrook Quarter Phase I. Exclusive of Twinbrook Quarter Phase I, same property revenue increased $2.3 million primarily due to (a) higher commercial base rent of $1.2 million and (b) higher expense recoveries of $0.7 million. Same property net operating income was favorably impacted by $1.1 million due to the lease up of Twinbrook Quarter Phase I. Exclusive of Twinbrook Quarter Phase I, same property net operating income increased $1.4 million primarily due to higher commercial base rent of $1.2 million. Shopping Center same property net operating income for the 2026 Quarter totaled $37.4 million, a 4.6% increase compared to the 2025 Quarter. Shopping Center same property net operating income increased primarily due to (a) higher base rent of $1.3 million and (b) lower credit losses on operating lease receivables, net, of $0.4 million. Mixed-Use same property net operating income for the 2026 Quarter totaled $14.9 million, a 6.1% increase compared to the 2025 Quarter. Mixed-Use same property net operating income increased primarily due to the lease up of Twinbrook Quarter Phase I of $1.1 million. Exclusive of Twinbrook Quarter Phase I, Mixed-Use same property net operating income decreased $0.2 million primarily due to lower expense recoveries, net of expenses, of $0.2 million. One property, Hampden House, which commenced operations on October 1, 2025, was excluded from same property results. Reconciliations of (a) total revenue to same property revenue and (b) net income to same property net operating income are attached to this press release. Same property revenue and same property net operating income are non-GAAP financial measures of performance that management believes improve the comparability of reporting periods by excluding the results of properties that were not in operation for the entirety of the comparable reporting periods.
